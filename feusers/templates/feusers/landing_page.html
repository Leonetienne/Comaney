Questions

Is Comaney really free?

Yes. If that ever changes, existing accounts stay free. AI features only cost money if you add your own AI key, and that's something you set up on purpose.

How does AI Express Entry work?

Take a photo of a receipt or type what you bought. The AI fills in the form, and you check it before anything is saved. New accounts get a small free monthly budget to try it.

Is my financial data private?

Your data is never sold. When you use AI features, what you give them is processed by the AI model this instance uses. If you don't use them, nothing is sent. The public instance uses Anthropic's Claude.

How does splitting costs work?

Split with one person through Contacts, or with a group through Projects. Set who paid and how to divide it, and Comaney works out the fewest payments to settle up. The other person confirms once they've been paid.

What does the dashboard show me?

Your month at a glance: income, spending, what's paid, what's still due, and what's left to spend. Plus charts by category and tag. Move, resize or build your own cards.

Can I set my own start of month?

Yes. If you get paid on the 25th, your month can run from the 25th to the 24th. All totals follow it.

Can I export my data?

Yes, as a ZIP of CSV files from your profile. There's also a REST API, and AI assistants can connect through MCP with their own key and permissions.

I don't use Euro, can I still use Comaney?

Sure. Set any currency symbol you like: "$", "£", "¥" or even "WoW Gold".

Do I need my own server?

No, you can just use this one. If you'd rather host it yourself, there's a Docker image. The code is on GitHub.

What AI services can Comaney utilize?

The admin of each instance picks one: Claude, DeepSeek, or any OpenAI-compatible API such as Ollama. AI can also be turned off completely. See the docs.
